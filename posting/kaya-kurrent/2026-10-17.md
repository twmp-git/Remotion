> 🇻🇦 Pope Leo XIV is recovering well after surgery for lung cancer, the Vatican said on Thursday.
> 
> Surgeons at Gemelli Hospital in Rome removed the lower part of his right lung on Tuesday, 13 October. The operation took about 90 minutes and had no complications, according to the Holy See Press Office.
> 
> The Vatican said tests during the operation confirmed lung adenocarcinoma, a common type of lung cancer. The Pope had announced on Sunday that doctors had found a small nodule in his lung at a very early stage.
> 
> On Thursday morning, the Vatican said he had a good second night, got up and had breakfast. Blood tests and a chest X-ray were 'within the expected range' for this kind of surgery. He is walking around his room, doing breathing exercises and eating normally. On Wednesday he said Mass in a room prepared for him on the hospital's 10th floor.
> 
> The Vatican says he is still expected to stay in hospital for about a week.
